Elf and elfa unlock together; every player can choose either. No purchases or speed/streak requirements. Hints and corrections count. These are rewards from saved village exercises during the Christmas window, 1 November–7 January; owned characters remain usable all year. The first full collection now has 27 Christmas rewards: twelve decorative/accessory items, twelve avatars and three badges. Scarf removal is a separate free control. The combined pending catalog has 97 entries; year-round collections remain separate and unchanged.

Elio/Stella are playable avatars, independent of Birillo. Birillo remains the optional prank visitor from 8 December, not an inventory character and not automatically added to a player's avatar choice.

## Finding and using them

Personaggi → 🎅 Personaggi Natale filters just the twelve actors. Natale still contains the whole festive collection; the original Personaggio tab remains. Free preview never grants or equips an unowned actor. Owned choices can immediately enter kart, football and space from the filtered tab. The selected avatar survives reload and remains wearable after closure.

Each actor uses original local Three.js primitives. Elves have pointed ears, different costumes and hair; Santa has a beard, red coat and cap; Nonna Natale has bun/glasses; Biscottino has a cookie silhouette, icing and buttons; polar bear and friendly yeti have winter bodies, with soft horns/fur for the latter; silver reindeer has a distinct frosted palette and antlers; the toy nutcracker has uniform and drum, without weapons. Native hats yield to equipped hats; ordinary accessories remain compatible. Compact passport/presence/class icons recognize every new ID and use native emoji portraits rather than an old sprite.

The rendering families are reused where appropriate, with original per-character geometry/palettes. This is not a claim of nine unrelated character rigs, speech interaction or photo-realistic animation. No external assets, service, credential, role or live profile change is introduced.

## Validation

The dedicated browser suite previews all nine new models without spending/unlocking, earns thirty synthetic exercise replies over ten fixture dates, then uses all nine owned actors in 27 game scenes. It checks distinct family geometry markers, saved identity/portrait mapping, native/equipped hat compatibility, four viewport widths and permanent use after closure. The real API suite checks progressive rewards at all ten thresholds and existing isolation/retry gates. Existing original avatars, festive missions, visitor metadata and normal priced cosmetics remain covered by regressions.

Local synthetic tests only. Full cumulative release CI, real-device validation, publication and live checks remain outstanding. [Synthetic renderer showcase](review/christmas-cast.png) presents nine additions; Rudy, Pip and Fiocco remain in the earlier showcase.
